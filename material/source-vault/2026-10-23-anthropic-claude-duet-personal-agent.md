## Summary

Anthropic has introduced Claude Duet, a standalone personal agent app built entirely around Opus 5.5. The pitch is simple: Duet runs on its own cloud computer, keeps working 24/7 even when your devices are shut, and takes proactive actions instead of waiting for prompts. Think morning briefs, calendar tracking, meeting booking — the grunt work of a workday, handled without you watching.

Duet inherits memory, skills, and connectors from the Claude app, and it can pay for things via a one-time virtual card through Stripe's Link wallet. Anthropic is being careful with the sharp edges: any deletion, payment, signature, or outgoing message requires your sign-off first. Usage counts against your normal Claude limits, and the research preview is rolling out in waves to waitlisted US Max and Pro subscribers.

The personal agent space is crowded and mostly undifferentiated. What makes Duet interesting is the model underneath — Opus 5.5 is arguably the strongest model shipping right now, and Duet is the only agent built entirely around it. Grok Bot already borrows Claude for some tasks; Duet cuts out the middleman.

## Key Points

- Standalone app powered by Opus 5.5, running on its own cloud computer — works even when your devices are off
- Carries over memory, skills, and connectors from the Claude app
- Payments via one-time virtual card through Stripe's Link wallet
- Safety gates: deletions, payments, signatures, and outgoing messages all require explicit user sign-off
- Research preview, rolling out in waves to waitlisted US Max and Pro subscribers; counts against normal Claude usage limits
