## Summary

Higgsfield says it crossed $1 billion in annualized revenue run rate in September, 18 months after launching its AI-native creative suite. Read the fine print: run rate means the last four weeks of revenue multiplied by 13 — a projection, not cash collected over a year. Still, the trajectory is steep, roughly 20x growth since September 2025, and the company says it has been breaking even on a cash-flow basis for the past couple of months.

The numbers behind it: 32M+ users across 238 countries, nearly 1M paying subscribers, and contracted business revenue up 10x since June. The platform powers campaigns for 390 of the Fortune 500. The US leads, followed by South Korea, Germany, India, the UK, Brazil, and France.

The demand engine is direct-to-consumer advertising. Ad production is inherently iterative — make a campaign, test variants, localize winners, keep generating as performance data lands. That loop turns AI video from a one-time tool into a subscription-plus-usage habit. Which explains the business model shift: subscriptions fell from over 90% of revenue to just over 60%, with on-demand generation making up the rest. The $400M Series B in August ($5.4B valuation, led by DST Global) now looks like it priced this growth in.

## Key Points

- $1B+ annualized run rate (last 4 weeks x 13), ~20x growth since September 2025
- 32M+ users in 238 countries; nearly 1M paying subscribers; 390 of Fortune 500 as customers
- DTC advertising is the growth engine — iterative ad production drives repeat usage
- Revenue mix shifting: subscriptions down from 90%+ to ~60%, on-demand usage growing
- $400M Series B at $5.4B valuation (DST Global); claims cash-flow break-even in recent months
